# Playbook — daniel

What I've learned doing the work here — I apply these every time. Newest last.
- 2026-09-29 — [pricing / free tier] When deciding free tier vs trial, first list each competitor's free tier (caps, what's missing), then size ours against it plus our cost-to-serve. A no-free stance in a market where incumbents give CRM away free needs an explicit reason. (from T-004)
- 2026-09-29 — [shipping with missing inputs] When an external input slips (captures, market data), ship the doc on its due date with those cells marked UNVERIFIED and a dated reconciliation task, rather than holding the whole deliverable. Make the dependency and its date explicit in the doc. (from T-004)
- 2026-09-29 — [pricing / free tier] Cost a free tier per tenant as Marcus's marginal infra plus support minutes at a stated loaded rate. Ship it with a hard tenant cap, a worst-case monthly ₹ figure, and explicit kill thresholds (e.g. <5% conversion by day 60, >30 support min/tenant/mo). (from T-004)
